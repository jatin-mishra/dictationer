Notes at the back

Questions writers ask

Will the AI change my story?

No. It writes down what you say, fixes spelling and punctuation, and suggests edits only when you ask. You approve every change.

Which languages?

Hindi and English at launch, including mixing the two in one sentence. Tell us the language you write in when you join; it decides which language comes next.

Can I publish on Amazon or Notion Press?

Yes. You get a print-ready PDF and an EPUB. If you use a generated cover on Amazon KDP, Amazon asks you to disclose it, and we'll show you how.

Is my voice data safe?

Your recordings are private, you can delete them anytime, and they are never used to train AI.

When does it launch?

First Edition writers get access first. We'll message you before anyone else.
